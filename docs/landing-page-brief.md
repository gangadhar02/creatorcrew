## 6. Core features (benefit-led)

> Group these into a features section. Each has a **headline (benefit)** + **sub
> (what it is)**. ✅ = live today, 🔜 = on the roadmap (tease as "coming soon").

### ✅ Save & organize everything in one place
Pull in your Instagram saves and bookmarks automatically. No more lost screenshots — every piece of inspiration lives in one searchable library.

### ✅ AI that understands your content (Vision + Transcripts)
Every saved reel and image is analyzed by AI — it reads the visuals, pulls transcripts, and tags the content — so you can search and understand *why* something works.

### ✅ Discover what actually performs
Find top-performing posts and creators in your niche. Spot **outliers** — the posts that massively over-performed a creator's baseline — so you model what works, not what's average.

### ✅ Profile Analyzer
Analyze any public Instagram profile in seconds: typical reel views, engagement, and breakout posts. Understand any creator's playbook.

### ✅ Chat with an AI that knows your taste
A creative copilot that's aware of your saved content and your voice. Ask it to ideate, remix a post, write a hook, or break down why a reel went viral. It can fetch a post's transcript or run a fresh vision analysis on demand. Attach images and PDFs.

### ✅ Your Voice, captured
Extract your writing voice and style once, then have every idea and draft generated in *your* tone — not generic AI mush.

### ✅ Infinite canvas boards
Collect, arrange, and connect content on a freeform whiteboard. Paste any Instagram link to drop it in as a card, sketch and add notes, group ideas into campaigns, and go fullscreen for a distraction-free creative space.

### ✅ Ideation & content pillars
Turn saved inspiration into a pipeline of content ideas, organized by your content pillars.

### 🔜 Link-in-bio
A beautiful, customizable link-in-bio page — built into the same tool.

### 🔜 DM automation
Auto-reply to comments and DMs, run comment-to-DM funnels, and turn engagement into leads — like ManyChat, bundled in.

### 🔜 Generate carousels & videos
Go from idea to finished carousel or short-form video with AI generation — all in your style.

## 10. Brand, tone & visual direction

- **Tone:** confident, creator-native, a little playful. Speaks *to* creators, not down to them. Short, punchy sentences. Benefit-first.
- **Avoid:** corporate jargon, "leverage synergies," generic SaaS-speak.
- **Visual vibe:** modern, clean, creative-tool aesthetic (think Linear / Notion / Framer crossed with a creator app). Generous whitespace, bold type, soft gradients, rounded cards, real product screenshots/mockups, subtle motion.
- **Mode:** support light and dark; the app itself is dark-mode-friendly.
- **Imagery:** app UI mockups (canvas, chat, cards), creator-flavored visuals, avoid stocky corporate photos.
